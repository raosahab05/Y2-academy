GENERAL HEALTH INFORMATION

Precautions & Discussion Points

Useful points to consider when reviewing your health report.

Important: These points provide general information only. They do not diagnose a medical condition or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
📋

Review Your Report

Check the parameter values together with the reference ranges provided by the laboratory.

🔎

Look at Trends

When previous reports are available, compare changes over time rather than looking at one result alone.

🧑‍⚕️

Discuss Concerns

Discuss unusual results, persistent concerns or symptoms with a qualified healthcare professional.

📅

Follow-up

Follow-up testing should be considered according to professional medical advice and individual circumstances.

Questions to Discuss With a Healthcare Professional

You can use these questions when discussing your report.

01

What do the values outside the reported reference range mean in my situation?

02

Do any of these results require follow-up testing?

03

Could any medicines, diet or lifestyle factors affect these results?

04

When should these parameters be checked again?

05

Are there any symptoms or changes that I should discuss promptly?

⚕️

Professional Medical Advice

If you have significant symptoms or are concerned about your health, seek appropriate medical care rather than relying only on automated report interpretation.
