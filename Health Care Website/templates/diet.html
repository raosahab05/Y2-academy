SYSTEM WORKFLOW

How Report-Based Guidance Works

The system reads structured parameters extracted from the uploaded report, checks their classified status, and uses relevant results to display general dietary and lifestyle information.

📄
1. Report Upload

The user uploads a laboratory report in a supported format.

🔍
2. Parameter Analysis

Relevant laboratory parameters are extracted and classified.

🥗
3. Guidance Generation

Relevant general dietary and lifestyle information is selected.

👨‍⚕️
4. Professional Discussion

Important or persistent concerns should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.
